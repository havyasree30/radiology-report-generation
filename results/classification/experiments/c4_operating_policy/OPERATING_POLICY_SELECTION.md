# C4: final operating-policy selection and No Finding consistency

_Generated by `scripts/c4_write_docs.py` from the stored C4 artifacts. Validation data only. The locked test split was not opened; no model was trained, no inference was rerun and no bootstrap was repeated._

## 1. Experiment objective

C2 compared score-based ranking; C3 produced two candidate threshold sets. C4 decides which **binary operating policy** should feed the downstream retrieval and report-generation stages, taking into account not only per-class accuracy but also how many findings are passed on per image and whether the final binary output is logically consistent.

## 2. Model and dataset

- **Model:** DenseNet-121, 14 independent sigmoid outputs (multi-label); loss = √-weighted BCE (C2-B, provisional). Checkpoint `results/classification/experiments/c2b_sqrt_weighted_bce/checkpoints/best.pt`, SHA-256 `6645ed44be7cb7d4cf4679b1fd561c7ac36be63f964ea320666199883126794b`, epoch 7.
- **Data:** 28,671 frontal CheXpert validation images from 9,530 patients; 14 observations in the fixed order of the project; uncertain entries are excluded from every metric and blank entries are scored negative.
- **C3 reproduction:** counts identical and metrics within 1e-12 for all three C3 policies; scores and Youden predictions in the C3 per-image file are bit-identical (True).
- **Terminology:** outputs are *sigmoid scores* (model scores); they are not calibrated probabilities.

## 3. Compared operating policies

1. **Threshold 0.50** for every class.
2. **Youden J** per-class thresholds (C3).
3. **F1-optimal** per-class thresholds (C3).
4. **F1-optimal + No Finding consistency rule:** No Finding is positive only if its own score exceeds its threshold **and** none of the 12 pathology observations is positive. Only the final binary No Finding output can change; scores and all other predictions are untouched.

Definitions used throughout: *pathology* = the 12 observations other than No Finding and Support Devices (used by the rule and the contradiction rate; Support Devices is excluded because the source labels allow No Finding together with a device: 1,126 of 2,538 validation No Finding images also have Support Devices positive); *abnormal findings* = all 13 observations other than No Finding (used for finding counts and the 13-class macro).

## 4. Evaluation metrics

Precision = TP/(TP+FP); recall = TP/(TP+FN); specificity = TN/(TN+FP); F1 = 2TP/(2TP+FP+FN); balanced accuracy = (recall + specificity)/2. Macro values are unweighted means over classes. A positive prediction is `score ≥ threshold`. FP per TP = FP/TP.

## 8. No Finding consistency

**Table 4. No Finding before and after the rule (F1-optimal thresholds).**

| Policy | TP | FP | TN | FN | Precision | Recall | Specificity | F1 | % images predicted No Finding | Contradiction (% of images) | Contradiction (% of predicted No Finding) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| F1-optimal (raw) | 1447 | 1703 | 24430 | 1091 | 0.459 | 0.570 | 0.935 | 0.509 | 11.0 | 2.6 | 24.0 |
| F1-optimal + No Finding rule | 1184 | 1211 | 24922 | 1354 | 0.494 | 0.467 | 0.954 | 0.480 | 8.4 | 0.0 | 0.0 |

- **Problem:** with F1-optimal thresholds No Finding is positive together with at least one pathology on 2.6% of images (755; 24.0% of images predicted No Finding). The same quantity is 3.1% at 0.50 and 25.8% under Youden. The validation labels contain 0 such cases.
- **Effect of the rule:** contradictions fall to 0.0%. No Finding precision rises 0.459 → 0.494, specificity 0.935 → 0.954; recall falls 0.570 → 0.467 and F1 0.509 → 0.480. The images predicted No Finding drop from 11.0% to 8.4% (label prevalence 8.9%).
- **Sensitivity analysis:** also suppressing No Finding when Support Devices is positive would remove all contradictions under the broader definition, but No Finding recall would fall to 0.239 (F1 0.321), because devices are common alongside truly normal findings. The narrower pathology definition is therefore used. Under the broader definition the selected policy still has 4.0% images with No Finding and Support Devices both positive, which the labels also permit.

## 9. Downstream implications

The retrieval query is built from the positive findings, so every false-positive finding becomes query content and can pull in reports about conditions the image does not show. Youden's thresholds would pass on a mean of 5.1 findings per image against 2.3 in the labels, with 45% of images above five, and 28 false alarms per correct call for the rarest class. The F1-optimal policy passes on 3.4. A logically consistent No Finding output also avoids sending the retrieval and generation stages a statement of normality together with listed abnormalities. These are mechanisms inferred from the counts above; the effect on retrieval quality and report content has not been measured.

## 10. Final operating policy

**Selected: F1-optimal per-class thresholds + the No Finding consistency rule** (`final_operating_policy.json`). The criteria were weighed qualitatively, without a composite score:

| Criterion | 0.50 | Youden | F1-optimal | F1 + NF rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Macro F1 (14 / 13 classes) | 0.430 / 0.425 | 0.399 / 0.398 | **0.452** / **0.447** | 0.449 / 0.447 |
| B. Macro precision | **0.426** | 0.321 | 0.396 | 0.398 |
| C. Macro recall | 0.448 | **0.754** | 0.543 | 0.535 |
| D. Rare-class false positives per TP | 2.0–6.5 | 6.6–28.1 | 2.9–7.5 | 2.9–7.5 |
| E. Mean findings/image (truth 2.31) | **2.48** | 5.12 | 3.43 | 3.43 |
| F. No Finding contradiction (% of images) | 3.1 | 25.8 | 2.6 | **0.0** |

- **Youden is rejected** for downstream use: it has the highest recall but the lowest precision and macro F1, 5.1 findings per image, and the largest rare-class and No Finding contradiction burden.
- **0.50 is not selected** although it has the best macro precision and the finding count closest to the labels: its recall is 0.45, and for the rare classes 0.11–0.29, so most true rare findings never reach retrieval.
- **F1-optimal + rule is selected** as the most balanced policy that also satisfies the consistency requirement: macro F1 0.449 (vs 0.430 at 0.50), macro recall 0.535 (vs 0.448), macro precision 0.398 (vs 0.426), zero contradictions. Raw F1-optimal has a marginally higher macro F1 (0.4515 vs 0.4495) but 2.6% contradictory images; the cost of the rule falls entirely on No Finding (recall 0.570 → 0.467, F1 0.509 → 0.480).
- **What the choice does not achieve:** the policy still passes on about one more finding per image than the labels contain, and rare-class precision remains low (§7). It is a validation-set operating point, to be revisited if the loss, the model or the score calibration changes.

Final-policy file checks: scores bit-identical to C2-B (True), raw predictions equal the threshold rule (True), final predictions equal the rule applied to raw (True), only No Finding differs between raw and final (True). Figure 8 shows the final thresholds.

## 11. Limitations

- Thresholds were selected on the same validation data used to evaluate them, so threshold-dependent numbers are validation operating-point performance, not independent estimates.
- The locked test split has not been evaluated; it remains untouched until the whole classifier configuration is frozen.
- Sigmoid scores have not been calibrated; absolute score levels differ between classes.
- The loss (√-weighted BCE) was chosen provisionally from a single-seed screening study; a different final loss requires re-fitting all thresholds.
- Blank labels are scored as negative, so precision may understate true precision for findings that were present but not reported.
- The effect on retrieval quality and generated reports has not been measured.
- The No Finding rule trades No Finding recall for consistency; whether that is the right trade for report generation is untested.

## 12. Conclusion

On the validation set, F1-optimal per-class thresholds with a deterministic No Finding consistency rule gave a more balanced precision-recall trade-off than either the fixed 0.50 threshold or Youden's J (macro F1 0.449, recall 0.535, precision 0.398), passed on 3.43 findings per image, and removed No Finding contradictions. Rare-class precision remains low under every policy.
