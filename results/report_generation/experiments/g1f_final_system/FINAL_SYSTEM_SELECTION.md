# Final report-generator selection

_Generated by `scripts/g1f_04_write_docs.py` from the stored G1 and G2 validation artifacts; validation data only._

## Decision

The final report-generation architecture is **G1 Single-Agent RAG**. G2 (structured multi-agent RAG) is not selected. G2 is a negative-result experiment and is not modified, rerun or repaired here.

## Factual basis (validation set, 378 clinical-subset studies; paired bootstrap, 1,000 resamples)

| Metric | G1 single-agent RAG | G2 multi-agent RAG | G2 - G1 (95% CI) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Finding F1 | 0.383 | 0.382 | -0.001 (95% CI -0.038 to +0.034) |
| Finding precision | 0.411 | 0.359 | -0.052 (95% CI -0.092 to -0.011) |
| Finding recall | 0.358 | 0.407 | +0.049 (95% CI +0.010 to +0.090) |
| Macro finding F1 | 0.215 | 0.312 | +0.096 (95% CI +0.042 to +0.147) |
| Classifier FP propagation | 0.405 | 0.831 | +0.426 (95% CI +0.358 to +0.490) |
| Classifier TP retention | 0.718 | 0.932 | +0.214 (95% CI +0.138 to +0.287) |
| Hallucination rate | 0.257 | 0.381 | +0.124 (95% CI +0.087 to +0.164) |
| Omission rate | 0.381 | 0.339 | -0.042 (95% CI -0.069 to -0.021) |
| Normal recall (report state) | 0.911 | 0.776 | -0.135 (95% CI -0.185 to -0.089) |
| Abnormal recall (report state) | 0.581 | 0.720 | +0.140 (95% CI +0.093 to +0.188) |
| ROUGE-L | 0.247 | 0.103 | -0.143 (95% CI -0.154 to -0.134) |
| Mean seconds per study | 3.7 | 17.6 | 4.8 times |

- Finding F1 is the same (G1 0.383, G2 0.382); G2 has lower precision (0.411 to 0.359).
- Classifier false-positive propagation is 0.405 for G1 and 0.831 for G2, and the hallucination rate 0.257 versus 0.381; both are reliably worse for G2.
- G2 improved recall (0.358 to 0.407), true-positive retention (0.718 to 0.932) and abnormal recall (0.581 to 0.720), but largely by propagating classifier-positive findings: its false-positive propagation is 0.831, close to the rule-based baseline B0 (1.000), whose report simply repeats the classifier output.
- G2's verifier did not discriminate supported from unsupported classifier findings: 35.3% of the classifier positives it labelled supported and 35.2% of those it labelled unsupported match the reference.
- G2's critic had negligible effect: it added or removed a stated finding in 2 studies (2 removals, 0 additions in the clinical subset).
- Runtime: about 3.7 s per study for G1 and 17.6 s for G2 (4.8 times), with up to three sequential model calls.
- G2's lower measured copying is partly a consequence of much shorter reports (mean 8.0 versus 23.9 words) and is not treated as a reason for selection.

## What is known about G1 and what the selection does not claim

G1 remains an imperfect system: when the classifier output is abnormal, its report reads as normal in 29.8% of studies; it copies 29.5% of its sentences from its own Top-5 on average (G1 analysis); rare-finding true-positive retention is weak. G1 is selected because, on the validation evidence, it is the better-grounded of the two architectures at similar F1 and much lower cost, not because it is clinically adequate. The system is a research prototype, not an autonomous diagnostic system, and the locked end-to-end test has not been opened.
