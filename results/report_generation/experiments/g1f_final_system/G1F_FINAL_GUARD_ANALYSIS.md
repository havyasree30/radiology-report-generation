## 1. Purpose and rule

G1 and G2 wrote a normal-looking report for all 49 studies in which the frozen classifier produced no positive label and No Finding was not positive. These studies must not be interpreted as normal. G1F adds a deterministic guard in front of the frozen G1 pipeline (`src/system/guard.py`, SHA-256 `1f0b340cc71cf261de84f4b19f290c99568a69760dc757d57c62d2c68c9397ee`); it is a system-logic correction, not a classifier, retrieval or generation experiment. The routing state `system_interpretation_state` (normal, abnormal or indeterminate) comes from the classifier output only and is never inferred from generated prose.

| Path | Condition | Action |
|---|---|---|
| A, normal | No Finding positive and no pathology label positive (Support Devices is not a pathology label, as in the frozen C4 rule) | frozen G1 normal behaviour; stored G1 report reused |
| B, abnormal | any other study with a positive label | frozen G1 Single-Agent RAG report reused |
| C, indeterminate | no label positive and No Finding not positive | no retrieval, no model call, fixed message below |

`FINDINGS: Model output is indeterminate for this study.`  
`IMPRESSION: Automated preliminary interpretation could not be established. Radiologist review is required.`

**Support Devices decision.** 9 validation studies have Support Devices as their only positive label; 8 of them also have No Finding positive (allowed by C4, which states that a device does not suppress No Finding) and are routed to Path A; 1 has No Finding negative and keeps its stored G1 report on Path B. Under a strict reading in which a device-only study without No Finding is abstained, Path C would be 50 studies; under a reading in which any positive label is pathology, the counts would be 261 / 237 / 49. The empty-output definition (no positive label at all) was chosen by the project owner before the freeze so that Path C is exactly the 49 empty classifier outputs reported in G1 and G2.

## 5. Abstention analysis (Path C; descriptive)

49 studies (9.0% of 547) are abstained; 24 have a reference (6.3% of the clinical subset) and 25 have none. Of the 24 with a reference, 9 (37.5%) are reference-normal and 15 (62.5%) reference-abnormal; abstention covers 8.1% of all reference-abnormal and 4.7% of all reference-normal studies. Reference findings among the abstained reference-abnormal studies (19 instances): Lung Opacity 8, Cardiomegaly 6, Atelectasis 2, Support Devices 2, Fracture 1 (rare). Rare findings account for 1 of these 19 instances (5.3%) against 49 of 285 (17.2%) in all reference-abnormal studies; at the study level 1 of 15 abstained and 45 of 171 decided reference-abnormal studies have a rare finding (Fisher exact p = 0.12). Rare findings are therefore not over-represented among abstentions in this validation set, with very small counts. The routing rule was not changed in response to these numbers.

## 6. Routing state versus report prose

The displayed state comes from routing, but the reused G1 report prose can disagree with it: on Path B the prose reads normal in 71 of 229 studies (31.0%) and abnormal in 158; on Path A the prose reads normal in 269 of 269. This is the abnormal-to-normal collapse of G1 documented earlier, not a new effect, and it was not changed because the G1 prompt and reports are frozen. An application must therefore show the routing state next to the prose and treat a mismatch as a known limitation, not as a decision.

## 7. Final pipeline and intended application behaviour

```
Chest X-ray -> preprocessing -> frozen DenseNet-121 -> calibrated 14-label outputs -> C4 binary decisions -> deterministic routing guard
  Explicit No Finding            -> frozen G1 normal path
  >= 1 pathology positive        -> top-3 classifier query findings -> hybrid dense + BM25 RRF -> Top-5 IU evidence reports -> frozen G1 MedGemma Single-Agent RAG -> preliminary Findings + Impression
  Empty / uncertain output       -> deterministic INDETERMINATE output (no retrieval, no LLM)
```

For an indeterminate study the interface must show **Automated interpretation unavailable — radiologist review required.** and must not show "No abnormality detected", "Normal X-ray" or any fabricated preliminary diagnosis. The Streamlit application has not been built.

## 9. Limitations

- The guard abstains on empty classifier outputs only; it cannot detect a wrong but non-empty output, and it does not repair the abnormal-to-normal collapse or the copying of G1 reports.
- 49 validation studies (9.0%) are abstained, including 9 reference-normal studies; abstention reduces coverage and shifts those studies to radiologist review.
- The empty-output definition treats Support Devices as a non-pathology label for explicit normal studies (as in C4) and keeps a device-only study without No Finding on the abnormal path; other readings give 50 or different path counts and were not evaluated further.
- Decided-case recalls and the abstention composition rest on small counts (24 abstained studies with a reference, 19 finding instances).
- Validation data only; one run of a small quantised local model; the locked retrieval and end-to-end test remains unopened.
