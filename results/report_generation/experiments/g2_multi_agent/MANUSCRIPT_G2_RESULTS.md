# Results: structured multi-agent RAG (G2)

## Generation and checks

All 547 of 547 studies completed without failure; 49 empty-output studies skipped Agent 1. Stored B0, G1, G1A and G1B results were reproduced exactly, and the leakage checks passed (no retrieved text in Agent 2 or 3 messages; no reference text in any message).

## G1 versus G2

On the 378 clinical-subset studies, finding precision was 0.411 (95% CI 0.354 to 0.469) for G1 and 0.359 (95% CI 0.309 to 0.408) for G2; recall 0.358 (95% CI 0.300 to 0.416) and 0.407 (95% CI 0.349 to 0.467); F1 0.383 (95% CI 0.330 to 0.435) and 0.382 (95% CI 0.333 to 0.430); macro F1 0.215 and 0.312 (Table 2, Figure 1). Reports with at least one hallucinated finding were 25.7% and 38.1%, with an omission 38.1% and 33.9% (Figure 2). Paired differences G2 minus G1 (Table 3): precision -0.052 (95% CI -0.092 to -0.011), recall +0.049 (95% CI +0.010 to +0.090), F1 -0.001 (95% CI -0.038 to +0.034), hallucination rate +0.124 (95% CI +0.087 to +0.164), omission rate -0.042 (95% CI -0.069 to -0.021), FP propagation +0.426 (95% CI +0.358 to +0.490), TP retention +0.214 (95% CI +0.138 to +0.287), normal recall -0.135 (95% CI -0.185 to -0.089), abnormal recall +0.140 (95% CI +0.093 to +0.188), ROUGE-L -0.143 (95% CI -0.154 to -0.134), BLEU-4 -0.033 (95% CI -0.037 to -0.029) and the METEOR variant -0.173 (95% CI -0.186 to -0.162). G2 met 4 of the six predeclared criteria relative to G1 (recall preserved (not reliably lower than G1); classifier TP retention preserved (not reliably lower than G1); abnormal recall preserved or improved (not reliably lower than G1); copying from the retrieved reports reduced) and did not meet: classifier FP propagation reduced relative to G1; hallucination rate reduced relative to G1. G2 is therefore not declared an improvement over G1 on this validation set.

## False positives, retention and normal/abnormal consistency

Classifier false-positive propagation was 1.000 for B0, 0.405 for G1 and 0.831 for G2; true-positive retention 1.000, 0.718 and 0.932 (Figure 3). Abnormal recall was 0.726 for the classifier, 0.581 for G1 and 0.720 for G2, and normal recall 0.729, 0.911 and 0.776 (Figure 4); when the classifier output was abnormal the report was normal in 29.8% (G1) and 0.0% (G2) of studies (difference -0.298 (95% CI -0.368 to -0.233)). Pooled true-positive retention over the six rare findings: B0 1.000, G1 0.333 (7 of 21), G2 draft 0.810, G2 0.810 (17 of 21); other findings: G1 0.802, G2 0.958. G2 minus G1 retention is +0.476 (95% CI +0.263 to +0.722) for rare and +0.156 (95% CI +0.076 to +0.240) for other findings; rare-finding retention changed reliably.

## Agent contributions

Agent 1 labelled 190 classifier positives supported, 22 partially supported and 142 unsupported in the clinical subset; the shares matching the reference were 35.3%, 0.0% and 35.2% (Table 4, Figure 5). Agent 1's validated support status did not discriminate between correct and incorrect classifier findings: 35.3% of 'supported' and 35.2% of 'unsupported' classifier positives match the reference (a difference of no practical size). The critic approved 56.3% of reports unchanged and modified 43.7% (239 of 547); in the clinical subset it removed 2 findings (2 not in the reference, 0 in the reference) and added 0 (0 in the reference, 0 not). Final G2 minus Agent 2 draft: F1 +0.001 (95% CI +0.000 to +0.003), recall +0.000 (95% CI +0.000 to +0.000), hallucination rate -0.003 (95% CI -0.008 to +0.000), FP propagation -0.008 (95% CI -0.022 to +0.000), TP retention +0.000 (95% CI +0.000 to +0.000). Of 34 retrieval-only candidates, 10 reached the draft and 10 the final report; G1 stated 68 findings beyond the classifier output (26.5% reference-supported) and G2 17 (41.2%) (Table 5, Figure 6).

## Copying and runtime

The copied-sentence rate against the study's own Top-5 was 29.5% for G1 and 0.0% for G2 (-29.5 points (95% CI -33.3 to -26.0)); whole-report copies 24.1% and 0.0% (-24.1 points (95% CI -27.9 to -20.7)); repeated-sentence rate 0.4% and 4.5%. G2 needed 17.6 s per study versus 3.7 s for G1 (4.8 times; Table 7).

## Interpretation

Single-model, single-prompt-set, single-run validation result; the 4-billion-parameter model's critic and verifier are weak components, the agent roles share one model, and no result here shows clinical benefit. Additional measured limitations: Agent 1 support status did not separate correct from incorrect classifier findings; Agent 1 output was truncated for 10.4% of studies with context; the critic changed a stated finding in 2 studies; G2 reports are terse; empty classifier outputs were still written as normal reports. G2 reports are much shorter than G1 reports (mean 8.0 versus 23.9 words; 89.5% of G2 sentences have fewer than six words and cannot register as copied, against 65.1% for G1), so the measured copying reduction is partly a consequence of terse output and not only of the agent separation; the repeated-sentence rate rose. Excluding the studies with truncated Agent 1 output left the G2 minus G1 conclusions unchanged (post hoc sensitivity analysis in the artifacts). The classifier, retrieval configuration and generator model were unchanged, the locked retrieval-test partition was not opened, and the final application has not been built.
